    EMBER COURT

    A duel of cards against Queen Cinder

    Flame beats Thorn Thorn beats Tide Tide beats Storm Storm beats Flame

    Each round you both play one card. Higher power wins the round and strikes for the difference — plus 2 when your element beats theirs. Play the same element twice in a row for a +2 Flow bonus. First to bring the other to 0 life wins the court.

    Mouse or keys 1–5 to play · H for rules · M to mute

    How to play

    • Goal: reduce Queen Cinder from 30 life to 0 before she does the same to you.
    • Each round you both play one card face down, then reveal together.
    • Power wins: the higher power takes the round and deals damage equal to the difference (minimum 1).
    • Element wheel: Flame > Thorn > Tide > Storm > Flame. Winning with the beating element deals +2 damage.
    • Flow: play the same element as your last round for +2 power.
    • Abilities: Flame cards Burn (+2 damage on a win) · Tide cards Soak (heal 3) · Thorn cards Barbs (3 damage back if you lose) · Storm cards Surge (+3 power after a loss) · Mirror Wisp copies the rival's power — two Wisps meet at 5 · Fate Jester wins ties.
    • Draw: you draw one card each round. When the deck runs out, the discard pile is shuffled back in.
    • Read the Queen: she saves big cards when ahead and strikes hard when behind — bait her, then answer.
